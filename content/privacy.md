At **Field Notes**, we respect your privacy and believe in zero-tracking digital publishing.

## 1. Zero Tracking & No Analytics Cookies

Field Notes does not use tracking cookies, cross-site trackers, advertising beacons, or third-party analytics scripts. We do not collect or profile reader identity, browsing histories, or personal behavioral metadata.

## 2. Server Logs & Edge Hosting

Like all websites hosted on static edge infrastructure, basic web server logs (such as IP address, user-agent string, and requested page URL) may be generated by the hosting network for essential network security, DDoS prevention, and routing purposes. These logs are not combined with personal profiles or retained for marketing.

## 3. Newsletter Subscription

If you choose to subscribe to the optional monthly email dispatch:
- Your email address is stored solely for delivering monthly essay notifications.
- We never sell, share, or monetize subscriber lists.
- You can unsubscribe at any time using the one-click link included at the bottom of every dispatch.

## 4. Local Storage Usage

This site uses browser `localStorage` solely for saving your light/dark theme preference locally on your device.

## 5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy, feel free to reach out via our [About & Contact](/about/) page or by emailing `hello@example.org`.
